      METHODOLOGY & DATA

      How Intrinsic works.

      The data

      Live public-market data is pulled from Yahoo Finance: quote and chart data, annual and trailing fundamentals, analyst targets, company profile and a live sector peer set. The server caches each ticker for 15 minutes and peer sets for one hour. If live data is unavailable, a saved or bundled snapshot is clearly labelled.

      • Price: delayed by at least 15 minutes; some exchanges longer.
      • Fundamentals: trailing-twelve-month and up to five reported fiscal years.
      • FCF: operating cash flow minus capital expenditure, then optionally less stock-based compensation.
      • FX: financial-statement currencies are converted into the quote currency when Yahoo provides a rate.

      The seven lenses

      Discounted cash flow
      A 5–15 year free-cash-flow projection with growth fading to a terminal rate, discounted at WACC, less net debt.
      Earnings power value
      Normalized after-tax operating earnings capitalised at WACC, with no growth. A conservative floor.
      Peer P/E
      Current EPS × the median P/E of up to six live sector peers. Falls back to a static sector benchmark when a peer set is unavailable.
      EV / EBITDA or P/B
      Operating companies use EBITDA × the peer median EV/EBITDA, less net debt. Banks and insurers use book value per share × the peer median P/B instead.
      Revised Graham formula
      EPS × (8.5 + 2g) × 4.4 / AAA yield. A 1970s heuristic, capped and treated as low confidence.
      Graham number
      √(22.5 × EPS × book value per share). Mostly a balance-sheet cross-check.
      Dividend discount
      A two-stage dividend model. Only weighted when the payout ratio is meaningful.

      The blend and confidence score

      Models are weighted by relevance and reliability, then normalized. Banks and insurers shift weight toward earnings and book value and away from DCF and EV/EBITDA; they receive a peer P/B lens instead. If free cash flow is negative, DCF drops out. If a dividend is immaterial, DDM is shown but not weighted. The headline is a weighted average; the bear/bull range is the weighted average of each model's own range.

      The confidence score combines model coverage, data completeness, dispersion between the lenses, DCF terminal-value dependence, live peer support and the quality red-flag count. It describes confidence in the valuation estimate, not confidence in future returns.

      Important limits

      This tool does not know management quality, competitive threats, litigation, accounting quality or macro shocks. DCF terminal values are sensitive to small input changes. Peer sets are sector-level, not a hand-curated comparable-company analysis; when live peers are unavailable, static sector benchmarks are used. Treat every output as a starting point for research, not a verdict.
